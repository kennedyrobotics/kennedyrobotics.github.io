Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. KR Fit has no account and doesn't send your workouts, weight or Apple Health data anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Can KR Fit use my smart scale?
Yes, through Apple Health. If your scale's app (for example Fitdays+) saves weigh-ins to Apple Health, open KR Fit's Profile tab and tap Connect Apple Health. Your readings then appear on the Weight tab. KR Fit only reads them; they stay in Apple Health on your iPhone.

How do workout templates work?
On the Workouts tab, tap Add starter templates for Push, Pull, Legs and 5×5, or tap New template to make your own with target sets and reps. Tap a template to start a workout from it. To reuse a workout you've already done, open it and tap Save as template.

How are personal records worked out?
A set is a record when it beats your heaviest weight, or your best estimated one-rep max, for that exercise. The first time you do an exercise sets the starting point, so it isn't counted as a record.

Is my data backed up?
KR Fit's data is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Deleting the app deletes its data from the phone.

How do I switch between kilograms and pounds?
Open the Profile tab and change Units. Your existing entries are converted for display.

Are the calorie and BMI figures accurate?
They are estimates from standard population formulas (BMI and Mifflin–St Jeor), intended as a general guide.

Not medical advice. KR Fit is a fitness and weight log. Its figures are estimates and are not a substitute for advice from a doctor, dietitian or other health professional. Check with one before starting a new diet or exercise program.
