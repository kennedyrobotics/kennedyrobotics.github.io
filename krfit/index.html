Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Weigh-ins, your profile and Apple Health data stay only on your iPhone. Workouts, templates and settings are on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account so your Apple Watch and other devices see them. KR Fit has no account and never sends your data to us. See the privacy policy.

How do I use KR Fit on Apple Watch?
KR Fit installs on a paired Apple Watch with the iPhone app (if it doesn't, open the Watch app on your iPhone and install it from there). While a workout is running on your iPhone, the watch shows the exercise and set you're on: adjust the weight or reps and tap Log set, and your iPhone updates straight away. With no workout on your iPhone, start an empty workout or a template on the watch. Tap the weight or reps and turn the Digital Crown, then tap Log set; it's pre-filled from last time. Your sets appear on your iPhone through iCloud, usually within a minute.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose KR Fit. Small shows this week's training days and your streak; medium adds your last workout. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, then the widget area.

My workouts aren't appearing on my other device.
Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that they're online. Opening KR Fit on each device helps it catch up. Weigh-ins and your profile don't sync by design.

Can KR Fit use my smart scale?
Yes, through Apple Health. If your scale's app (for example Fitdays+) saves weigh-ins to Apple Health, open KR Fit's Profile tab and tap Connect Apple Health. Your readings then appear on the Weight tab. KR Fit only reads them; they stay in Apple Health on your iPhone.

How do workout templates work?
On the Workouts tab, tap Add starter templates for Push, Pull, Legs and 5×5, or tap New template to make your own with target sets and reps. Tap a template to start a workout from it. To reuse a workout you've already done, open it and tap Save as template.

Can I plan my week?
Yes. On the Plan tab, tap Edit plan and put templates and activities (such as BJJ) on the days you train. Anything you miss stays on the list to catch up that week. Workouts recorded by your Apple Watch, such as Martial Arts, count automatically if you've connected Apple Health.

Can I edit my plan on a computer?
Yes. On the Plan tab, tap Export or import, then Export plan and templates. Open the file in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, make your changes, save it as CSV, and use Import plan and templates to bring it back. KR Fit shows what will change before anything is replaced. You can also export your workout history as a backup.

How does the Nutrition tab work?
Add the foods you eat to each meal with their calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat; KR Fit remembers them for next time and shows your totals against suggested targets. Your food log stays on your iPhone only.

How are personal records worked out?
A set is a record when it beats your heaviest weight, or your best estimated one-rep max, for that exercise. The first time you do an exercise sets the starting point, so it isn't counted as a record.

Is my data backed up?
KR Fit's data is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), and your workouts are also kept in iCloud if you use it. Deleting the app deletes its data from the phone, but not the workouts in your iCloud account.

How do I switch between kilograms and pounds?
Open the Profile tab and change Units. Your existing entries are converted for display.

Are the calorie and BMI figures accurate?
They are estimates from standard population formulas (BMI and Mifflin–St Jeor), intended as a general guide.

Not medical advice. KR Fit is a fitness and weight log. Its figures are estimates and are not a substitute for advice from a doctor, dietitian or other health professional. Check with one before starting a new diet or exercise program.
