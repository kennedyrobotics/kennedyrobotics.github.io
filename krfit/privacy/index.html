KR Fit

Privacy policy

KR Fit for iPhone · Effective 21 November 2026 (updated for the weekly plan, nutrition log, file export and more Apple Health data)

In short: KR Fit does not collect, store or share any of your personal data on our servers. Your weigh-ins, profile, food log and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone. If you use iCloud, your workouts and weekly plan also sync through your own private iCloud account, so they appear on your Apple Watch and other devices. We can't see any of it.

Who we are

KR Fit is made by Kennedy Robotics, the trading name of Robert Kennedy, a sole trader in Australia (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at kennedyrobotics@bigpond.com.

What the app stores, and where

To work, KR Fit stores the information you enter:

  • workouts: exercises, sets, repetitions, weights and notes, and your workout templates
  • your weekly plan and the activities you log, such as BJJ or a run (duration, intensity, how it felt, notes)
  • body-weight entries and their dates
  • your food log: meals, foods, servings, calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat, and the foods you save
  • profile settings: units, height, birth year, sex, activity level and goal weight
  • app settings: appearance, rest timer length, units, weight step, activity types and plate calculator settings

Body-weight entries, your food log and your profile are stored only on your iPhone, using Apple’s on-device storage. They are never synced to iCloud.

Workouts, templates, your exercise list, your weekly plan and activities, and app settings are stored on your iPhone and, if iCloud is turned on for KR Fit, in your own private iCloud storage (see iCloud).

Files you export: if you choose Export in the app, KR Fit creates a file with your plan and templates, or your workout history, and saves or shares it wherever you choose (for example iCloud Drive, OneDrive or email). After that it's your file, handled by the service you chose.

None of this information is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we have no copy of it.

What we collect

Nothing. KR Fit has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). The only internet use is syncing with your own iCloud account through Apple; KR Fit never sends your data to us or to anyone else, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, KR Fit keeps your workouts, templates, exercise list and app settings in sync across your devices, including the KR Fit app on your Apple Watch. They are stored in your private iCloud database, which belongs to your Apple Account and is managed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Kennedy Robotics cannot read it. It uses your iCloud storage, not ours.

Your weigh-ins, your profile and anything read from Apple Health are deliberately left out of iCloud sync and stay on your iPhone.

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for KR Fit in the Settings app (tap your name, then iCloud). KR Fit keeps working on your iPhone without it.

Widgets and Apple Watch

KR Fit’s Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your training days this week, your streak and your last workout. To do that, the app keeps a small summary in storage shared only with its own widget, on your iPhone. Lock Screen widgets can be seen by anyone who can see your Lock Screen; you can remove them at any time.

The Apple Watch app gets your workouts and templates through your iCloud account, and sends the sets you log on the watch back the same way. While a workout is running on your iPhone, the iPhone also sends the exercise, weight and reps you're on straight to your paired Apple Watch (and the watch sends back changes and logged sets), using Apple's direct iPhone-to-watch connection. This stays between your own two devices. If you allow notifications, it reminds you when your rest time is up. Those reminders are created on the watch itself and contain only the exercise name.

Backups

Your iPhone may include KR Fit’s data in its normal backups, for example iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, under your own settings, not by us.

Information Apple provides to us

If you choose to share crash reports or usage statistics with app developers in your iPhone settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may give us that information. It does not include the workouts or weights you enter. We use it only to fix problems and improve the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app. Deleting a workout or template also deletes it from iCloud and your other devices. Deleting KR Fit from your iPhone deletes all of its data from the phone, but not the copy of your workouts in your iCloud account: delete those in the app first, or remove KR Fit’s data from your iCloud storage in the Settings app (tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your storage). Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

KR Fit is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Apple Health

If you choose Connect Apple Health, KR Fit asks your permission to read these items from Apple Health:

  • weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass and waist circumference (for example from a smart scale), to show your trends
  • height, date of birth and sex, to fill in your profile when you tap Fill profile
  • workouts recorded by your Apple Watch or other apps, so they count in your plan and streak
  • active and resting energy and steps, to estimate how many calories you burn
  • resting heart rate, heart rate variability and sleep, for a recovery guide

KR Fit only reads this information while you use the app, on your iPhone. It does not write anything to Apple Health, does not copy your Health data into its own storage, and never sends it anywhere: not to us, not to iCloud, not to your Apple Watch, not to anyone else. It is not used for advertising or sold. You choose which items KR Fit may read, and can change that at any time in the Health app under Sharing ▸ Apps ▸ KR Fit.

Health information

KR Fit’s calorie, nutrition target, BMI, body fat and recovery figures are estimates for general guidance, not medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If we change how KR Fit handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
