KR Fit

Privacy policy

KR Fit for iPhone · Effective 15 November 2026 (updated for Apple Health)

In short: KR Fit does not collect, store or share any of your personal data on our servers. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

KR Fit is made by Kennedy Robotics, the trading name of Robert Kennedy, a sole trader in Australia (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at kennedyrobotics@bigpond.com.

What the app stores, and where

To work, KR Fit stores the information you enter:

  • workouts: exercises, sets, repetitions, weights and notes, and your workout templates
  • body-weight entries and their dates
  • profile settings: units, height, birth year, sex, activity level and goal weight

This information is stored only on your iPhone, using Apple’s on-device storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we have no copy of it.

What we collect

Nothing. KR Fit has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). The app does not use the internet to send your data anywhere, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Backups

Your iPhone may include KR Fit’s data in its normal backups, for example iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, under your own settings, not by us.

Information Apple provides to us

If you choose to share crash reports or usage statistics with app developers in your iPhone settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may give us that information. It does not include the workouts or weights you enter. We use it only to fix problems and improve the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app. Deleting KR Fit from your iPhone deletes all of its data from the phone. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

KR Fit is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Apple Health

If you choose Connect Apple Health, KR Fit asks your permission to read these items from Apple Health:

  • weight and body fat percentage (for example from a smart scale), to show your weight trend
  • height, date of birth and sex, to fill in your profile when you tap Fill profile

KR Fit only reads this information while you use the app, on your iPhone. It does not write anything to Apple Health, does not copy your Health data into its own storage, and never sends it anywhere: not to us, not to iCloud, not to anyone else. It is not used for advertising or sold. You choose which items KR Fit may read, and can change that at any time in the Health app under Sharing ▸ Apps ▸ KR Fit.

Health information

KR Fit’s calorie, BMI and body fat figures are estimates for general guidance, not medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If we change how KR Fit handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
